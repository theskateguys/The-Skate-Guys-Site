# Homepage Trust Rollout

Date: 2026-07-07
Status: Draft placement for review

## Purpose

This rollout keeps the homepage focused on booking while bringing the people and community proof closer to the top of the page.

The full coaches section and full testimonials section are still part of the page. This change adds a short trust band after the hero so first-time visitors see real TSG faces and a real community quote before moving into merch, updates, events, and packages.

## Placement

The new band appears directly after the hero:

1. Hero: `Book a Class` and `See Upcoming Events`
2. Trust band: coach/community imagery, short quote, links to coaches and reviews
3. Merch spotlight
4. Updates/events/classes and the rest of the homepage

## Why This Fits The Direction

- The homepage remains a decision page, not a full information dump.
- The main action stays `Book a Class`.
- The brand still feels human, local, and community-led.
- Coaches and testimonials are sharpened, not removed.

## Review Checklist

1. Confirm the selected coach/community images feel like the right first trust signal.
2. Confirm the testimonial excerpt is the right one to show near the top.
3. Decide whether the trust band should feature one coach, multiple coaches, or a stronger group photo.
4. Keep the full coaches and testimonials sections unless TSG explicitly chooses a shorter homepage later.
5. Make sure this section does not push booking too far down on mobile.

## Future Options

- Swap in the Canoe Bay group photo when the approved asset is available.
- Rotate the quote seasonally, but keep one quote only near the top.
- Add a compact coach row only if it does not overload the first scroll.
