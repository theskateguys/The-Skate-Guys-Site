# TSG Merch Mockup Rollout

Date: 2026-07-07
Status: Draft review assets, not final product photography

## Purpose

This rollout adds clean temporary merch visuals to `merch.html` so the store section feels real while final product photos or Canva-approved product renders are still pending.

The mockups preserve the current brand direction:

- dark premium sport-business base
- TSG cyan, pink, and gold accents
- existing The Skate Guys logo
- no stock people, no AI people, no unsupported product/shipping/discount claims

## Temporary Assets Added

- `assets/merch/mockup-tshirt-logo.svg`
- `assets/merch/mockup-hoodie-logo.svg`
- `assets/merch/mockup-bucket-hat-logo.svg`
- `assets/merch/mockup-keychain-logo.svg`
- `assets/merch/mockup-socks-logo.svg`

These are intentionally reusable category mockups. They are meant to improve the visual quality of the draft merch grid without pretending to be final printed samples.

## Where They Are Used

- Bucket Hat uses the bucket hat mockup.
- Hoodie products use the hoodie mockup.
- Keychain uses the keychain mockup.
- T-shirt products use the T-shirt mockup.
- Socks use the socks mockup.
- Classic T-Shirt keeps the existing committed community promo image.

## Review Checklist Before Publishing

1. Confirm the logo placement feels right on dark garments.
2. Confirm whether each product should use a generic category mockup or a product-specific design.
3. Replace SVG mockups with final Canva, product-photo, or Shopify-exported images when approved.
4. Keep image alt text descriptive and honest: use `mockup` until final product photos exist.
5. Avoid adding any merch discount, free delivery, shipping, or availability claim unless TSG confirms it.

## Suggested Rollout

### Preview

Use these mockups on the audit branch only and review the merch page on mobile and desktop.

### Approval

TSG chooses one path:

- keep the temporary category mockups for launch
- replace with Canva-generated product-specific mockups
- replace with real product photos

### Finalization

Before launch, rename final assets clearly and remove `Temporary` from alt text only if the visuals are approved as final product imagery.

## Notes From Homepage Guidance

The attached research recommends keeping the site focused on conversion clarity:

- one dominant action: `Book a Class`
- one secondary action: `See Upcoming Events`
- short proof and pathing instead of information overload
- real TSG visuals wherever possible
- lightweight media and reduced motion support

These merch mockups support that direction by making the merch path feel visually complete without adding heavy scripts, frameworks, stock imagery, or distracting motion.
