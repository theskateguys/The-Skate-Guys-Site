The Mindset

Elite Means Commitment,
Not Status.

Elite doesn't mean you're better than anyone else. It means you're serious enough about your skating to invest in your own growth and back the movement at the same time.

You show up. You put in the work. You build with discipline, consistency and purpose. Elite gives you the structure to do that with real support behind you.

Elite Is

  • A paid commitment membership
  • Structure & accountability
  • Real perks for real commitment
  • Backing the movement
  • A path for serious skaters

Elite Is Not

  • Paid access to the free chat
  • Unlimited free everything
  • A guaranteed leadership role
  • Monthly auto-pay subscription
  • Control over TSG decisions
