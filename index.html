Quick Answers

FAQ

Is it beginner friendly? +
Yes — we teach from first stand-up to advanced dance moves. No experience needed whatsoever.
Do you accept all ages? +
Yes — kids, teens, adults, and family groups are all welcome. We teach by skate level, not age.
Do I need my own skates? +
Bring your own if you have them. Otherwise request rental skates when booking and include your shoe size — rental skates are $60 TTD/hour.
How do I pay? +
We'll confirm your spot via WhatsApp and share secure payment options then. Simple and straightforward.
Are classes crowded? +
No — class sizes are capped intentionally to keep floors safe and coaching personal. We'd rather have a waitlist than a crowded rink.
Can I try before committing to a plan? +
Absolutely. Book a single $60 TTD drop-in first. You can also ask about our next free Community Meet Up.
What if the month is full? +
We'll add you to the waitlist and send a free community session invite so you can experience the vibe while waiting for a spot.
