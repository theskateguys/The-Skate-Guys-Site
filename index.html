Quick Answers

FAQ

Is it beginner friendly?

Yes. We teach from first stand-up to advanced dance moves.

Do you accept all ages?

Yes—kids, teens, adults, and family groups. We teach by skate level.

Do I need my own skates?

Bring your own skates; otherwise request rental skates and add your shoe size.

How do I pay?

We’ll confirm your spot and share secure payment options in WhatsApp.

Are classes crowded?

No—bookings are limited to keep floors safe and coaching personal.

Can I try before joining?

Yes—ask about our next free Community Meet Up or book a single skate class.
