Rescheduling, cancellations and credit
  • At least 24 hours' notice: the amount paid carries forward as credit.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice or no-show: no automatic credit; genuine emergencies are reviewed individually.
  • TSG/coach cancellation or unsafe weather/venue: the full amount paid carries forward as credit, without penalty.
  • Credit expires one calendar month from the original scheduled session date. If the matching date does not exist, use the last day of that month.
  • If TSG cannot offer a replacement before expiry, we extend the credit to give you a reasonable opportunity to attend and record the agreed new expiry.
  • Transfers require TSG approval, subject to participant suitability and skate availability.
  • Credit rather than refunds.
